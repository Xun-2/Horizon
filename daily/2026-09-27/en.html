Background

DeepSeek Elastic Compute (DSec) is presented as sandbox infrastructure for large-scale agentic training, where AI agents can execute workloads in isolated environments. The platform reportedly provides a unified SDK across function-call, container, microVM, and full-VM sandbox backends, allowing different isolation and execution mechanisms to be managed through one interface.
